⚡ PEDAGOGICAL BOTTLENECK: DETERMINISTIC BRITTLENESS VS. GENERATIVE OVER-ASSISTANCE
01 CLASSICAL ITS PARADIGM

RULE-BASED, BKT & IRT SYSTEMS

✓ Low Latency (<50ms): Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) and Item Response Theory execute deterministically on client/edge.
✗ Brittle Pedagogical Heuristics: Incapable of understanding nuanced student prose or novel mental models.
✗ Zero Semantic Synthesis: Cannot formulate contextualized metaphors or personalized Socratic analogies for adult professionals.
FAST BUT SEMANTICALLY BLIND
02 GENAI TUTOR PARADIGM

MONOLITHIC GENERATIVE LLMS

✓ Rich Reasoning & Socratic Dialogue: Multi-turn semantic explanations, cross-domain grounding, and contextual tutoring.
✗ Prohibitive Latency & Token Burn: Median first-answer latency ranges from 0.42s to 700s; consumes ~48,200 tokens/hr.
✗ Destroys Productive Struggle: Unguarded over-explanation terminates the learner's cognitive effort and shatters flow state.
EXPRESSIVE BUT SLOW & EXPENSIVE

03. THEORETICAL FOUNDATION
KAHNEMAN'S COGNITIVE DIVISION

DUAL-PROCESS THEORY IN PEDAGOGICAL AI

HOW EXPERT HUMAN TEACHERS OPERATE: FAST INSTINCTIVE REFLEX + DELIBERATE ASYNCHRONOUS REASONING

01 EDGE REFLEX ENGINE

SYSTEM ONE: AUTONOMOUS REFLEX

  • Fast, Parallel & Reactive: Design target <40 ms inference.
  • Monitors Micro-Telemetry: Keystroke intervals, cadence entropy, scroll jitter, dwell times.
  • Zero UI Disruption: Preserves immersion while learner is in productive struggle.
  • Action Set: Micro-nudges, syntax tips, IRT item calibration.
HIGH-FREQUENCY EDGE INFERENCE (POMDP)
02 SOCRATIC DELIBERATION

SYSTEM TWO: SOCRATIC REASONING

  • Slow, Serial & Deliberate: Transformer-based LLM.
  • Engaged Only Upon Impasse: Triggered when load exceeds threshold.
  • Preserves Learner Agency: Generates conceptual analogies; never dumps raw solution code.
  • Asynchronous RPC: Runs out-of-band to prevent UI locking.
SELECTIVE SOCRATIC INTERVENTION (GENAI)
"An expert human tutor does not deliberate over complex pedagogy at every keystroke. Subtle cues trigger instinctive reflex; deep deliberation engages only upon cognitive impasse."
